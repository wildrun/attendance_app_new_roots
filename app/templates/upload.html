What the app does with the file
  1. Reads every join and leave time, ignoring rows from other dates.
  2. Works out how long each person was actually in the session, counting overlapping devices once and treating gaps between rejoins as missed time.
  3. Matches people to your roster by email first, then by name.
  4. Shows you the results. Nothing is saved until you press the save button.
